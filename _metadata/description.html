This package includes various SBB Cargo International and SBB Cargo liveries. It contains over 15 liveries.

This repaint package includes, among others, the Alppiercer 1, 2, and 3, Shadowpiercer, and Silverpiercer liveries. It also includes special liveries such as the Nightpiercer, Goldpiercer, and Hollandpiercer.

Technical specifications:
  • Maximum speed: 160 until 200 km/h
  • Power: 6400 kW
  • Towing force: 300 until 350 kN
  • Availability: from 2014
